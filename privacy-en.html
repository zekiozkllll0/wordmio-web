Privacy Policy

2026-11-24

Summary

MindWidget needs no account, collects no personal data and contains no ads or tracking. Your data stays on your device. To improve the app, it sends anonymous usage statistics that cannot be linked to you (see below).

What is stored

On your device: the languages and level you chose, your daily goal, your progress (learned words, review dates, days you opened the app), your colour choices, your reminder setting and, if you add them, your first name, last name and profile photo. The profile photo never leaves your device.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to a server for it.

Widgets

The home and lock screen widgets get the words they show from the app through a shared space on your device. This data never leaves your device either.

Microphone and location

MindWidget does not use the microphone, location or contacts, and uses the camera only if you choose to take a profile photo.

Speech

Pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see which parts of the app are used and where people get stuck, the app sends anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), for example “a quiz was finished” or “the Premium screen was opened”, together with technical details such as the app version, iOS version, device model, language settings and level. Each installation gets a random number that is irreversibly transformed (hashed) on your device before it is sent. No name, e-mail, contacts, location, advertising identifier or anything you type is sent, and the signals cannot be linked to you. This data is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone else.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and use no advertising services. Only two services receive data, as described in this policy: TelemetryDeck (anonymous usage statistics) and RevenueCat (Premium purchase records).

Deleting your data

Me → Delete account removes your profile and all progress from this device. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

Children

The app collects no personal data, so it is suitable for users of all ages.

App updates

So that you get fixes quickly, the app asks the update server of Expo (the platform it is built with) whether a newer version exists. This request contains only technical information such as the app version and platform, no personal data.

Premium purchases

MindWidget Premium (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is bought through the Apple App Store. Apple handles the payment; your payment details never reach us. To check whether your Apple account has Premium, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), which receives the App Store purchase records together with a random app user ID, never your name, e-mail or payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Web version

In the web version, data is kept only in your browser's local storage.
