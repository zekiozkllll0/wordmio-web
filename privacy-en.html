Privacy Policy

2026-10-22

Summary

Wordmio needs no account, collects no personal data and contains no ads or tracking. Your data stays on your device.

What is stored

On your device: the languages and level you chose, your daily goal, your progress (learned words, review dates, days you opened the app), your colour choices, your reminder setting and, if you add them, your first name, last name and profile photo. The profile photo never leaves your device.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to a server for it.

Widgets

The home and lock screen widgets get the words they show from the app through a shared space on your device. This data never leaves your device either.

Microphone and location

Wordmio does not use the microphone, location or contacts, and uses the camera only if you choose to take a profile photo.

Speech

Pronunciation uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

Sharing

We do not share or sell your data and we use no analytics or advertising services.

Deleting your data

Me → Delete account removes your profile and all progress from this device. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

Children

The app collects no personal data, so it is suitable for users of all ages.

App updates

So that you get fixes quickly, the app asks the update server of Expo (the platform it is built with) whether a newer version exists. This request contains only technical information such as the app version and platform, no personal data.

Web version

In the web version, data is kept only in your browser's local storage.
