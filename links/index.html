Set it up

Lock Screen
  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area under the time.
  4. Pick MindWidget and choose a widget.
Home Screen
  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search “MindWidget”.
  4. Pick a widget and a size, then tap Add Widget.

4 widgets: shuffle words, shuffle sentences, a mini question and today’s 3 words. Tap them for the next one.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Three levels (A0–A2), tests and all four widgets are free.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

How often do words change?

Widgets show a new word on their own every 15 minutes, and a new one every time you tap.

Android?

iPhone only for now. Android is on the way.

What do you do with my data?

Nothing. We collect no data. Read the privacy policy.
